# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise code, not people.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints, experience levels and native languages.
- Give feedback on the work: what's wrong, why it matters, what would be better.
- Accept feedback the same way. Nobody's first patch is perfect.
- Assume good faith before assuming malice or incompetence.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind — including about someone's nationality, gender,
  sexuality, disability, religion, age or appearance.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately derailing issues and reviews.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages — and
when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **conduct@standbye.org**. Reports are handled confidentially and you'll get a
response. Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close threads, or ban an account, at their
discretion and in proportion to what happened.

Maintainers are held to this standard too — more so, not less.
